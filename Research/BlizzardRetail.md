# Blizzard Retail/Mainline UI research

Primary reference: [Gethe wow-ui-source, `live` branch](https://github.com/Gethe/wow-ui-source/tree/live). For example, the [Blizzard UIPanels Mainline TOC](https://github.com/Gethe/wow-ui-source/blob/live/Interface/AddOns/Blizzard_UIPanels_Game/Blizzard_UIPanels_Game_Mainline.toc) lists mainline-only and shared casting-bar resources. Exact modules, frames, and assets must be produced with the bundled scanners against a pinned checkout.

Retail is the development target only. Its APIs/assets are not evidence of WoW Forever compatibility.
